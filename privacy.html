Privacy Policy — PromptPics

Effective date: 30 October 2026.

PromptPics («صوري وبرومبتاتي») is a personal library for saving images together with the text prompts that describe them. This policy explains what the app stores, and what it does not.

Data stored on your device

Your images and prompts are stored locally on your own device: in the app's local storage on Android, and in the browser's local storage on the web page. Nothing leaves your device unless you turn on sync.

Sync (optional, only after you sign in)

If you sign in with your Google account and enable sync, the app copies your items into a private application folder inside your own Google Drive (the Drive appDataFolder). The app requests only the drive.appdata permission, so it can access only its own application folder. It cannot see, open, or change any other file in your Drive.

Sign-in and tokens

Sign-in uses Google OAuth. The access and refresh tokens are stored only on your device. Signing out of sync in the app revokes the app's access and deletes the stored tokens from the device.

What is never collected or shared

The app sends your images and prompts nowhere else. There are no advertisements, no analytics, and no tracking, and your data is never sold or shared with any third party. The developer never sees your library: sync goes directly between your devices and your own Google Drive.

Deleting your data

Deleting an item in the app removes it from your library on all synced devices; a small deletion marker may remain in the Drive application folder so other devices learn about the deletion. Uninstalling the app removes the local data on that device; copies already synced stay in your Drive application folder until you delete them there or revoke the app's access in your Google account.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised through the project's repository: github.com/playmurtada5/promptpics.

Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date above will be updated.
